Green chile cheeseburgers

34 places for a green chile cheeseburger, from roadside drive-ins to old bars. Each is a stop on New Mexico True's Green Chile Cheeseburger Trail, the state tourism department's own list, matched to an open listing at its address in fall 2026.

The state's own trail

New Mexico True, the state tourism department, keeps a Green Chile Cheeseburger Trail of some 49 stops. We list the ones we could tie to one open place at the trail's address: the Owl Bar and the Buckhorn in San Antonio, Sparky's in Hatch, Bob's Burgers on Central Avenue, Jerry's Cafe in Gallup and dozens more. Chains listed at "multiple locations" aren't tied to one place, so they aren't here. Say "Hatch" only where the place says it: New Mexico's Chile Advertising Act is strict about where chile comes from.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Albuquerque (4), Santa Fe (3), Ruidoso (2), San Antonio (2), Las Cruces (2), Arroyo Seco (1), Pinehill (1), Logan (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if the state tourism department's own trail (New Mexico True) or a dated 2025 or 2026 source named it and an open listing at its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
