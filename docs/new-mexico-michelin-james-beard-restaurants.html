New Mexico MICHELIN & James Beard restaurants

27 New Mexico restaurants recognized by MICHELIN or the James Beard Foundation. The MICHELIN Guide American Southwest lists 12 New Mexico places (five Bib Gourmand and seven Recommended restaurants); 17 places hold a James Beard honor (2 America's Classics, 3 finalists, 10 semifinalists and 2 winners).

What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide: its first American Southwest selection, revealed in August 2026, names twelve New Mexico places and no stars: five Bib Gourmands (Frontier in Albuquerque; Jambo Cafe, Paper Dosa, La Choza and Tia Sophia's in Santa Fe) and seven recommended restaurants. The other is the James Beard Foundation: America's Classics Mary & Tito's Cafe (2010) and Rancho de Chimayó (2016), Best Chef: Southwest winners Fernando Olea of Sazón (2022) and Mark Kiffin of The Compound (2005), the 2025 and 2026 finalists, Bow & Arrow Brewing, Mesa Provisions and Campo at Los Poblanos, and the semifinalists. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.

MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. New Mexico Eats is not affiliated with either.
