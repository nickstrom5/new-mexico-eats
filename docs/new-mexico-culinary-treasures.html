Culinary Treasures

25 restaurants that have stood the test of time and are still run by their families, from Albuquerque's Mary & Tito's to Las Vegas, Raton and Las Cruces. Each is on New Mexico True's Culinary Treasures list, matched to an open listing at its address in fall 2026.

Still family-run

New Mexico True's Culinary Treasures are independent restaurants still owned and run by their founding families or the people they chose. Albuquerque's Mary & Tito's Cafe, open since 1963, is a James Beard America's Classic (2010); the list runs to Charlie's Spic & Span in Las Vegas, Pappas' Sweet Shop and the Oasis in Raton, El Sombrero Patio Cafe in Las Cruces and Joseph's on Route 66 in Santa Rosa.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Albuquerque (5), Las Cruces (3), Los Ranchos de Albuquerque (2), Santa Rosa (2), Raton (2), Sunland Park (1), Las Vegas (1), La Mesa (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if the state tourism department's own trail (New Mexico True) or a dated 2025 or 2026 source named it and an open listing at its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

On your phone, New Mexico Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
