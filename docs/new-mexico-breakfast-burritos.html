Breakfast burritos

25 places for a breakfast burrito, smothered red, green or Christmas. Each is a stop on New Mexico True's Breakfast Burrito Byway, the state tourism department's own list, matched to an open listing at its address in fall 2026.

Ask for early accessThe free New Mexico Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Red, green or Christmas

New Mexico True's Breakfast Burrito Byway runs from Albuquerque's Frontier, across from the university, to Santa Fe's Tia Sophia's and The Pantry, Bernalillo's Range Café and Fina's Diner up in Chama. Frontier and Tia Sophia's are also MICHELIN Bib Gourmands. Chains listed at "multiple locations" aren't tied to one place, so they aren't here.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Albuquerque (6), Santa Fe (3), Bosque Farms (2), Los Ranchos de Albuquerque (2), Bernalillo (2), Mesilla (1), Belen (1), Los Lunas (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if the state tourism department's own trail (New Mexico True) or a dated 2025 or 2026 source named it and an open listing at its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

On your phone, New Mexico Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
